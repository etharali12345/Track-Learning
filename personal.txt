    PK (publishable key, pk_...):
        - is used in the app, on the user’s/client device. It’s what the app sends to identify itself to the service.
        - It’s designed to ship inside client apps. On its own it can only do what a public client is allowed to do: start a sign-in flow, show the sign-up UI. It can’t read other users’ data or manage your Clerk account.
        - fine as plain text? already visible to anyone, extract it by decompiling the app. The real protection comes from server-side checks
    SK (secret key, sk_...) 
        - used only on your backend server, never in the app. It’s for actions that must be trusted:
        - gives full admin access: read, create, or delete users, and so on. It must live only on a server you control.
